All three activity overviews default to progressive regional change: an all-region (six by default) slope of raw GPR-linked relative use, multiplied by six-region linear fit R², direction consistency and step evenness. Positive trends are first, negative trends last; near-flat and irregular trends lie between. “Strict monotonic ↑↓” excludes any local direction reversal from the ranking (score 0) without dropping the row. Choose the legacy endpoint ordering to reproduce existing exports; reaction and metabolite rankings use their own native regional means (reaction absolute relative flux use; metabolite processing), not gene-linked values. Region means and heatmap z-score colors are unchanged. This descriptive ranking is not proof of angular/radial specificity. Complete coverage: every supported feature is represented as a row in its overview. Genes without unambiguous GPR-based flux attribution are counted in the model inventory but are not falsely colored as inactive. Each overview has a searchable, readable row list below the full matrix. Activity summaries use the supplied saved FBA fluxes, not experimental measurements.

Methods and limitations

Angular: equal angles across normalized growth–production space. Circular: equal-occupancy bands in normalized radius. Circular distance is not Pareto-front distance. Gene scores describe GPR-linked reaction use, not gene expression; metabolites show processing proxies, not concentration; individual reactions show saved signed flux, not gene-level function.

Dataset loading is model-agnostic: use the included builder with any matched GEM JSON and exact precomputed reaction-flux matrix. No special antibody reaction IDs or species-specific analysis rules exist in the interface. The supplied antibody data are an example only.

This analysis explores saved FBA-optimal flux solutions across environmental conditions. It does not alone establish necessary pathways, regulation, or experimentally confirmed biology. The empirical frontier is across media, not a within-medium Pareto sweep.
